What we build

Commercial and institutional plumbing, built for the schedule.

As a plumbing specialty contractor, Eastern Mechanical focuses on new construction for the buildings communities depend on: commercial projects, schools and public facilities.

New Commercial Construction

Plumbing for new commercial buildings, planned around the drawings and the general contractor's schedule.

Schools & Education

Plumbing for school buildings, where systems need to be ready and dependable before students arrive.

Public & Institutional Buildings

Institutional plumbing for public buildings that are expected to serve their communities for a long time.

Also available

Remodeling

Plumbing for the renovation and remodeling of existing spaces, scoped to fit the building and the project.
